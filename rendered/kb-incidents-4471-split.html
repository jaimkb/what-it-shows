Where each stands

How each report was placed

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and these tenants write the day first. The date comes out with day and month swapped. Placed by the tenant’s locale, not by the intake tag, which guessed from the reporter’s browser and is often wrong.
  • hint: the report came through the booking form of a tenant on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type month first as told, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Same swapped date, different bug: the parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day-move: the desk read these as a date one day off, not a swap: Tuesday shown as Monday, one meeting on two days for people in two time zones. A month/day fix will not move them. Listed in desk-notes.json, and counted where the desk files them, under their tenant’s cause.

What changes on the desk
